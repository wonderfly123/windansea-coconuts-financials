    How we sell

    Three lanes. Harrison hunts the big and the recurring; Trent converts everything that comes in the door. Still unowned: servicing wholesale accounts after the close, and marketing beyond social.

    Wholesale

    Owner: Harrison

    Hotels, resorts, pool bars and restaurants that stock branded coconuts on a standing order.

    How it sells. Outbound. Harrison opens the account, sets pricing and reorder cadence.

    When it pays. Pays May to Sep. Accounts go quiet after summer and restart in spring, so every account signed in April is worth five months, one signed in August is worth two.

    Customers so far: Pelican Hill, Ritz Carlton Laguna, Omni San Diego, Evans Hotels, Torrey Pines Lodge, Walkabout, Coy's Produce.

    Where it shows up in the runway: The new accounts per month and value per account knobs in the Dashboard runway.

    Big hitters: tech conferences and brand activations

    Owner: Harrison

    Large one off activations: conferences, launches, corporate brand moments. Five figure tickets, the only revenue that shows up in winter.

    How it sells. Outbound and partnerships. Harrison owns the pipeline, proposals and close. These are the deals that decide whether the team makes it through January.

    When it pays. Year round, lumpy. Vegas conference season and Q4 corporate events are the winter opportunity.

    Customers so far: Currency Cloud ($67k), dbt Summit, AWS re:Invent (Glow), Vanta SF, Datafold, Macy's, Resorts World Las Vegas.

    Where it shows up in the runway: One off deals in the Dashboard runway, typed in by month.

    Inbound events: weddings, birthdays, corporate

    Owner: Trent

    Middle of the pack events that come to us: weddings, birthday parties, company parties, real estate and community events.

    How it sells. Inbound. Trent quotes, books, staffs and runs them end to end. Ads and organic social feed this lane.

    When it pays. Follows the San Diego event calendar, strongest April to October.

    Customers so far: Michael Kang wedding, Citreno corporate event, Clark Sandcastle, real estate events, AI on the Lot, Foodbeast.

    Where it shows up in the runway: The events multiplier from ads and organic in the Dashboard runway.
